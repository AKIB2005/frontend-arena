# Scholarship data inbox

Copy one template per scholarship. Add facts only after reading the official page.

## Template

```text
id:
name:
provider:
amount:
what the amount covers:
deadline: YYYY-MM-DD or rolling
levels: Class 10-12 | Undergraduate | Postgraduate | PhD
fields: Engineering | Medicine | Science | Commerce | Arts | Law | Any
categories: General | OBC | SC | ST | EWS | Minority
gender restriction: none or women
maximum family income:
minimum percentage:
states: Maharashtra | Karnataka | Gujarat | Delhi | Tamil Nadu | Uttar Pradesh | All
documents:
official URL: https://
date checked: YYYY-MM-DD
```

**Copy facts only from the official page.** Do not infer missing amounts, eligibility rules, deadlines, URLs, or document lists. Leave unknown fields blank.

After review, convert the entry to a typed object in `src/data.real.ts` without changing any value.
